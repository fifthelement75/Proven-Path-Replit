---
name: Meeting provider scope
description: Product requirement for simultaneous Meeting Intelligence providers.
---

A company must be able to retain several distinct Meeting Intelligence providers at once; choosing another meeting tool must not replace the first.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested more than one Meeting Intelligence option. HRIS and ATS were outside that request and should retain their single-provider behavior.

**How to apply:** Keep meeting-provider management independent, including disconnecting just the selected tool. Multiple workspaces for the same provider are a separate product decision, not implied by this request.
