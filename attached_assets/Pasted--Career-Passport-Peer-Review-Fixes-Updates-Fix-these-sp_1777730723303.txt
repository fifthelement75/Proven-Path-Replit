# Career Passport - Peer Review Fixes & Updates

Fix these specific issues with the peer review system:

---

## 1. Separate "Request Peer Review" Section

The Peer Review system should NOT be in the same place as "Add Review" (self/manager review).

**Create new navigation section:**
- Rename or add section called: **"Request Reviews"**
- Under this section, have two options:
  - **"Peer Review"** - for requesting soft skills feedback from peers
  - Keep "Add Review" for self-assessment and manager review only

**Why:** Self/Manager reviews cover HARD skills. Peer review covers SOFT skills. They are different processes and should be visually separated.

---

## 2. Manager Notification When Peers Complete

When peers complete their peer review, the **requesting manager** should be notified on the platform.

**Implementation:**
- Add a "Notifications" section in manager dashboard
- When peer submits review → Create notification record:
  - "Peer review completed for [Employee Name] by [Peer Email]"
  - "2 of 5 peers have completed their review"
- Show notification badge on manager's navbar
- Click notification → shows peer review results progress

**Database:**
```
Notification:
- id
- userId (manager receiving)
- message
- type (peer_review_completed)
- read (boolean)
- createdAt
```

---

## 3. Peer Review Form - Use Attached Template

The peer review form should follow this exact structure (based on attached Designer template):

**Header:**
"Peer Review"
Note: "Your answers are kept private and will not be shared publicly with the employee."

**Categories & Questions:**

### Category 1: Project and Team Management
Questions (each with scoring + optional comment):
1. Personal planning and delivery: Plans and delivers one's own work on time; communicates status and issues appropriately to key stakeholders
2. Track-level planning and delivery: Shows a clear understanding of the organization's planning and delivery process and can plan and estimate work effectively
3. Solution driven and proactive: Always seeks out support and collaboration from team members to share knowledge and remove barriers to complete tasks or goals
4. Team leadership: Effectively lead/mentors practice in core skills. Seeks out other team's strengths to improve their own work and build their own knowledge

### Category 2: Collaboration
Questions:
1. Persuasion and influence: Able to effectively build consensus around their practice solutions. Actively listens, understands, and when appropriate, incorporates other people's points of view into the proposed solution. Use a variety of communication strategies and tools, like workshops and design sessions, to achieve consensus
2. Working with cross-functional teams: Collaborates effectively with the triad and other stakeholders

### Category 3: Strategic Thinking
Questions:
1. Alignment with business goals: Effectively aligns their work with product and organization objectives
2. Customer-centric thinking: Applies research and customer needs to inform their decisions
3. Data-informed decision making: Uses data to validate their work

---

## 4. Scoring Scale

For EACH question, use this scoring (same as template):
- **Needs Improvement** - Not currently effectively using this skill to support the team in their objectives and is actively working to improve the skill
- **Meets Expectations** - Uses this skill effectively with their team and is actively working to improve their ability in this skill
- **Often Exceeds** - Able to effectively apply a wide range of strategies to a variety of challenges encountered on the team
- **Exceptional** - Models the way for fellow team members and is capable of mentoring others in this skill
- **N/A** - Not applicable

**Plus** optional comment field for each question.

---

## 5. Final Questions After All Scoring

After all skill questions:
- "Would you recommend this person for promotion?" (Yes / No / Unsure)
- "What are this person's key strengths?" (text area)
- "What areas should this person improve?" (text area)
